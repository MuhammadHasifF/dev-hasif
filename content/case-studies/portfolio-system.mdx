# Portfolio System

<Callout title="Goal" variant="info">
  Build a portfolio that feels like a product: bold typography, calm depth, scroll-driven motion, and a structure that can grow with new projects.
</Callout>

## Why a “system”?

Most portfolios fail in two ways:

- They look good but don’t scale as you add work.
- Or they scale, but feel generic and static.

This project treats the portfolio like a **design system + content system**.

## Design direction

- Dark, near-black surfaces (Obsidian / Graphite)
- Large typographic hierarchy (headline-first)
- Subtle glass + borders for depth (not heavy shadows)
- One accent gradient used sparingly for emphasis

## Motion rules (Apple-inspired, not noisy)

- Short durations, soft easing
- “Reveal” motion only when it supports hierarchy
- Scroll-driven hero fade and a time/progress indicator
- Respect reduced-motion preferences (easy to add later)

## Content model

Projects are the index. Case studies are the story:

- **Projects page**: searchable + filterable grid
- **Case studies**: MDX pages with frontmatter for metadata (tags, year, links)
- **Timeline**: chapters that highlight as you scroll

## Outcomes

- Strong first impression (hero + motion)
- Easy to add projects without redesigning the site
- Case study pages that support long-form storytelling
