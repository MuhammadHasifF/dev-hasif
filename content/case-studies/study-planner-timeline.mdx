# Study Planner Timeline

<Callout title="Problem" variant="warning">
  Traditional planners are task-heavy and don’t help you *see progress over time*. The goal was to turn study planning into a time-based narrative.
</Callout>

## Core interaction

- A timeline that you can scrub
- Milestones that expand into details
- Filters for modules / priority / deadline proximity

## UX decisions

- Reduce cognitive load with defaults and progressive disclosure
- Keep typography calm and readable on dark backgrounds
- Make “what’s next” obvious (a “Now” marker)

## What I’d ship next

- Import/export schedules
- Weekly review mode (reflection + adjustment)
- Better metrics: streaks, time spent, outcomes
